Rates

Amount per print = hours × hourly rate + filament grams × cost per gram (× qty). Rate changes apply to unpaid entries; reimbursed entries keep the amount they were paid at.

Tip: a 1kg spool at $25 ≈ $0.025/g. For hourly rate, a common approach is (printer cost ÷ expected lifetime hours) + electricity + wear.

Data & backup

Data lives in this browser on this device (localStorage) — GitHub Pages can't store anything server-side. Export a backup regularly, and import it on another device to move your data over.
